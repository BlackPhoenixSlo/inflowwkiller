How it works: this extension watches OnlyFans for the first signed /api2/v2/ request after you log in, grabs user-id, x-bc, x-of-rev, sign, time, user-agent + cookies, and packages them as a curl command. Each sign-in captures a fresh x-of-rev, so when the relay flags rev drift just log in again and paste the new curl.
