Installing

Four steps, about two minutes
  1. 1

    Download and open the .dmg

    Google Drive will warn that it can't scan a file this large. That's a size limit on Drive's scanner, not a finding — choose Download anyway.

  2. 2

    Drag Fastt into Applications

    The window that opens shows exactly that. Then eject the disk image.

  3. 3

    Open it — and expect macOS to refuse the first time

    !
    "Fastt" is damaged and can't be opened.
    You should move it to the Trash.
    Move to Trash

    The app is not damaged. This build isn't signed with an Apple Developer certificate, and macOS shows this same message for every unsigned app it hasn't seen before. Click Cancel — never Move to Trash.

    Open Terminal (⌘-Space, type "Terminal"), paste this line, press Return, then open Fastt again:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Fastt.app

    You only ever do this once, on this one app.

  4. 4

    Sign in

    First launch asks for a Server URL — leave it at https://fastt.lol — and your access token, which is the part after ?t= in your Fastt link. After that the app asks for your normal username and password, same as the website.

Before you rely on it

Known

Desktop notification pop-ups don't work yet. The unread count on the dock and taskbar is accurate and updates live — but the banner that slides in from the corner doesn't appear. Keep an eye on the badge. We're still on this one.

Note

The app doesn't update itself. When there's a new version you'll get a fresh link — install it over the top, no need to uninstall first. Your sign-in is kept.

Careful

For adding OnlyFans accounts, the Chrome extension is still the safer route. The app can capture a login itself (Menu → Add OnlyFans Account), but that path hasn't been proven on a real account yet. For an account that matters, keep using the extension.
