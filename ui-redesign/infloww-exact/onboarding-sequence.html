Hand-off AI Chatter
The drip stops the moment the fan replies — at the welcome or any step. AI Chatter picks up the thread in her voice and takes it from there. If all three nudges go unanswered she rests; a later reply restarts the cycle after a 72 h cool-off.
Every fan gets at most 3 nudges, at least 18 h apart, and never two automations in the same 12 h. The words are written fresh each time — tune the voice in Templates.
Advanced mode exact timing + who gets skipped — the drip runs fine without touching these

Who gets the sequence

Fans who've spent at least $1The drip is for fans who already paid once — freeloaders never get it.
Fans who engageThey've liked her content (brand-new fans count too).

Who skips it

Already chattingThe fan replied recently — AI Chatter owns the thread; the drip pauses 26 h.
Waiting on youThey have an unread message in — she owes a real reply, not a nudge.
Just contactedAny blast, nudge or AI message touched them in the last 12 h.
Restricted, muted or blockedSkip-list and blacklist fans never get the drip.
UndeliverableOnlyFans rejected a DM to them — the fan rests for 7 days.
Cold for 7+ daysA chat that's been dead a week gets no reminders.
