Advanced mode run the pipeline one job at a time · power notes

Run individual jobs

Sync vault (Collect all)
Mirrors your whole OnlyFans vault locally so browsing, search and every AI job are instant. Read-only sweep of OF — nothing is moved, sent or posted.
Describe all
Vision reads every new or changed item and writes description, tags and tier. Capped at 80% of the day's AI budget — anything left over rolls to tomorrow's pass.
Flags sweep
Re-checks flagged items and names the garment instead of judging it. Runs in the background — the page stays usable while it works.
Find duplicates
Spots re-uploads of media already in the vault. Duplicates get the ⧉ badge and are only flagged — OnlyFans has no hard delete.
Harvest OnlyFans search
Runs OnlyFans' own vault search across ~50 selling keywords and folds every hit into local search — so a search here finds items your AI tags missed. Read-only sweep; no fan is messaged.
Fix disagreements
Items where the describe pass and the flags pass contradict each other. You pick the reading that matches the picture — locked against future re-runs. Read-only until you apply a fix.

Recovered scripts

Upload bursts read back as shoots. Each batch is scored for direction — an escalating forward run, a reversed one (newest-first uploads, so the payoff sits at the top), or flat. Read-only and free: nothing is written until you apply.
