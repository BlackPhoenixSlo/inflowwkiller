Advanced mode manual sleep window, delay bounds, burst reset & the spend floor

Sleep window

Leave the times empty and the window is read off her own send history — currently 02:00–09:00. Anything you type here wins.
While she "sleeps", incoming messages wait for the morning. She wakes staggered over ~45 min, so 40 fans don't all get answered at 09:00:00 sharp — and she explains the gap herself.

Reply delay

Under 25 seconds is where almost no human lives — only 1.4% of her real replies land that fast. Past the maximum, the sale was already lost anyway.

How she covers a gap

Applies to

Broadcasts, welcome messages and scheduled sends keep their own pacing — reply rhythm only shapes her 1:1 AI replies.

Burst limits — fine tuning

The reset gap is measured on her own replies, not his — a fan who keeps typing never holds the burst open forever. Once an offer goes stale it drops him to the short "went cold" leash until he shows a fresh signal.

Spend floor — never short-leash a paying fan

A proven spender keeps a long leash even when he's momentarily gone quiet. Each rule lifts the cap for a fan who's paid at least this much in a rolling window — it only ever raises the leash, and the highest matching rule wins.
