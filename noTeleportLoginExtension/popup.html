How this avoids the teleport: normally, you log in from your home IP, OF mints cookies anchored to that IP, then the relay (on a proxy IP) starts sending signed calls — OF sees the account "jump" and flags it. This extension flips the order: the proxy is in place for the very first packet OF receives, so login and runtime egress from the same IP. Deactivate the proxy when you're done — it stays off for the rest of your browsing.
